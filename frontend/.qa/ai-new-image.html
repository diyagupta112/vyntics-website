Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers about AI use cases, delivery timelines, data security, model choices, and measurable value.

Can’t find the answer you’re looking for?

Reach out to us at contact@vyntics.com and we’ll be happy to help.

What do AI development services include?

They can include AI consulting, grounded knowledge assistants, tool-using agents, intelligent document processing, workflow automation, generative AI features, and LLM integrations inside existing software. The right scope starts with a specific business decision or task, not a model.

How do we know if our business is ready for AI?

You need a defined use case, an accountable owner, usable data or knowledge, access to the systems involved, and a measurable outcome. If those basics are missing, the honest first step is readiness work—not an AI build.

How long does it take to build an AI solution?

A focused assessment or pilot can take a few weeks. A production system with integrations, permissions, evaluation, security, and operational controls takes longer. Any precise estimate before reviewing the workflow and data is guesswork.

How much does AI development cost?

Cost depends on data readiness, integrations, security requirements, evaluation depth, user volume, and whether the work is a pilot or a production system. We scope those variables before proposing a budget instead of quoting a meaningless flat price.

Is our data safe when using AI?

It can be, when identity, permissions, retrieval boundaries, provider data policies, encryption, logging, retention, and deployment architecture are designed correctly. Security cannot be bolted on after sensitive sources are connected.

Which AI models do you work with?

We work with OpenAI and Azure OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, and appropriate open-source models, plus retrieval and orchestration platforms such as Pinecone, Qdrant, LangGraph, and LangChain.

How do you reduce hallucinations?

We constrain models with approved evidence, retrieval and reranking, structured outputs, validation rules, explicit refusals, citations, representative evaluation sets, and human review where an incorrect result carries material risk.

How do we know whether the AI is delivering value?

We agree on operating measures before implementation: task success, answer and citation quality, manual work removed, exception rates, cycle time, latency, adoption, and cost. Model benchmarks alone do not prove business value.
